# 🗺️ Step 2: Plan Your Channel
**⏱️ 2:00 → 6:00 (4 minutes)**

## 🎯 Goal

Leave this page with a **niche**, a **name**, and **10 video ideas**. Written down.

---

## 🧠 1. Pick your niche (2 options is fine)

For you, two strong candidates:

| Niche | Why it works | Your angle |
|---|---|---|
| **Rivals / Roblox** 🎮 | You know it cold; players search for guides | Loadouts, tips, updates, tier lists |
| **K-pop reactions** 🎤 | Fandoms rewatch and share everything | First-time reactions, rankings, news |

💡 Best move for a 12-year-old gamer: **start with Rivals.** Gaming is safer (no
face needed), and guides get searched forever. Keep K-pop as channel #2 later — or
mix in "gamer reacts to K-pop" as your spice.

---

## 🧠 2. Name your channel

Rules for a good name:

- ✅ Easy to **say** and **spell**
- ✅ Works as a **username** (YouTube, Roblox, Discord — same everywhere)
- ✅ Not locked to one game ("RivalsKing" hurts if you switch games)
- ❌ No numbers or underscores if you can avoid it (`JeffreyPlays` > `xX_jeffrey_293`)

**Test:** say it out loud. Could a friend find it by hearing it once?

---

## 🧠 3. The First 10 list

Write **10 video ideas now**, before you make video 1. Channels die because video 2
has no idea. Your first 10 for a Rivals channel:

1. Rivals in 60 seconds (complete beginner guide)
2. Top 5 weapons ranked
3. Best loadout for [your main]
4. 5 mistakes every new player makes
5. Update breakdown — what changed
6. I used the *worst* weapon for a whole match
7. Pro settings copy-paste
8. Ranking every map
9. Free-to-play vs paying — honest take
10. Q&A — ask me anything about Rivals

Notice: every one has a **clear promise**. Not "my gameplay #4" — nobody clicks that.

---

## 🧠 4. The One Viewer trick 🎯

Before filming, write one sentence:

> *"This video is for the Rivals player who keeps dying in the first 30 seconds."*

One viewer, one problem. Videos made "for everyone" are watched by no one.
