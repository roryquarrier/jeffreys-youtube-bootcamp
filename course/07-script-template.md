# ✍️ Script Template

Copy this into a doc before every video. Fill the boxes, film, done.

---

## 📋 Pre-flight

- **One viewer:** *This video is for the Rivals player who ______*
- **Promise:** After watching, they can ______
- **Thumbnail text (≤3 words):** ______
- **Title (≤60 chars):** ______

---

## 🎬 The script

### HOOK (0:00–0:12) — write it word for word

> *"______________________________."*

(Must contain: who it's for + what they get. No "hey guys welcome back".)

### BODY — max 3–4 points, one line each

1. **Point 1:** ______ *(show: gameplay clip of ___)*
2. **Point 2:** ______ *(show: ___)*
3. **Point 3:** ______ *(show: ___)*

### PAYOFF (last 15 sec) — write it word for word

> *"______________________________."*

(Must contain: the promise kept + where to go next.)

---

## ✂️ Edit checklist

- [ ] Hook lands in the **first 8 seconds**
- [ ] Every silence cut
- [ ] Gameplay visible during every explanation
- [ ] Music quiet under voice
- [ ] Watched it back once, **got bored nowhere**

---

## 📤 Upload checklist

- [ ] Title ≤ 60 chars, has a number/stakes/twist
- [ ] Thumbnail readable at phone size, 1 focal point
- [ ] First line of description = the promise
- [ ] "Made for kids" set correctly
- [ ] Scheduled for the regular slot
