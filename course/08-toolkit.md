# 🧰 The Free Toolkit

Everything below is free and standard among creators. You need nothing else to start.

---

## 🎥 Recording

| Tool | Use | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **OBS Studio** | Gameplay + voice | The industry standard; works on any PC |
| **Roblox's own recorder** | Quick clips | Fine for Shorts; OBS for real videos |
| **Phone voice memo** | Backup audio | Always record a spare track |

**OBS settings that matter:** 1080p, 30fps (60 if your PC can), and record a
**test clip first.** Nothing hurts like a perfect take that didn't save.

---

## ✂️ Editing

| Tool | Best for |
|---|---|
| **CapCut** | Starting out — cuts, music, captions, all easy |
| **DaVinci Resolve** | When you outgrow CapCut — pro colour and audio |

Rule: **learn 5 features deeply** (cut, text, music, zoom, speed) instead of 50
badly. Fancy transitions aren't why anyone subscribes.

---

## 🖼️ Thumbnails

| Tool | Use |
|---|---|
| **Canva** | YouTube thumbnail templates → swap text and image |
| **Screenshot from your video** | Better than stock — it's authentic |

**The 3-second test:** open your thumbnail on a phone, look away, look back. Do you
understand the video in 3 seconds?

---

## 📊 After upload

| Tool | Use |
|---|---|
| **YouTube Studio** (app or web) | CTR, retention, comments |
| **The 'top search terms' panel** | What people actually type — future video ideas |
