# 📈 Step 5: Grow & Stay Safe
**⏱️ 16:00 → 20:00 (4 minutes)**

## 🎯 Goal

The long game: what actually grows a channel — and the rules that keep *you* safe
while it does.

---

## 🧠 Part 1 — The growth rules that compound

### 1. The 20-video deal 📅
Judge nothing before 20 videos. Your first 10 are practice. Videos 10–20 are where
you find your style. Most creators quit at 3 — that's the whole trick: **don't.**

### 2. Watch your own analytics 📊
After each video, check two numbers: where CTR is low → fix thumbnail; where
retention drops → fix editing. Every video is a lesson, not a lottery ticket.

### 3. Reply to every comment (early on) 💬
Your first 100 viewers are worth more than your next 100,000. A channel with 5
loyal commenters beats one with 500 silent ones.

### 4. Steal like an artist 🎨
Find 5 Rivals/gaming channels you love. For each of their top videos ask: *why did
this work?* Copy the **structure** (hook! payoff!) — never the content.

### 5. Collaborate 🤝
Other small creators are colleagues, not competition. Duos and shout-outs are how
small channels grow together.

---

## 🧠 Part 2 — The money truth (honest version)

The **YouTube Partner Program** requires:
- **1,000 subscribers**, and
- **4,000 public watch hours** in 12 months **or** 10M Shorts views in 90 days
- You must be **13+** (and payment needs an AdSense account — adults handle this)

Reality check: that's month 6–18 of consistent work for most. **Don't start for the
money.** Start because you'd make videos anyway — that's the only motivation that
survives to video 50, where the money actually starts.

---

## ⚠️ Part 3 — Safety rules for a 12-year-old creator (non-negotiable)

| Rule | Why |
|---|---|
| **Your parents know and approve your channel** | Age rules, protection, and honestly better videos |
| **Never share**: real full name, school, address, phone, daily routines | Viewers include people you don't want knowing that |
| **No meetups** with viewers, ever, without a parent | People online are not always who they say |
| **Comments on = moderated** | Delete/block anything weird, immediately |
| **Don't read out hate comments on camera** | Feeds the trolls; hurts you |
| **It's "made for kids" or it isn't** — set it honestly | COPPA rules; wrong settings kill your comments and notifications |
| **Screen-time honesty** 🎮 | School first. Burnout kills more channels than the algorithm |

> 💬 The creators who last treat it like a **craft**. The ones who flame out treat
> it like a slot machine.
