# 🚀 Step 4: Upload Like a Pro
**⏱️ 12:00 → 16:00 (4 minutes)**

## 🎯 Goal

The video is made. Now the 15% that decides whether anyone sees it: **title,
thumbnail, timing.**

---

## 🧠 The two numbers that rule everything

| Metric | Meaning | Good looks like |
|---|---|---|
| **CTR** (click-through rate) | % who saw the thumbnail and clicked | 4–10% |
| **Retention** | % of the video actually watched | 40%+ |

- CTR = your **title + thumbnail** (decided *before* they watch)
- Retention = your **video quality** (Step 3)

The algorithm doesn't promote "good videos." It promotes videos **people click and
finish.** You now control both.

---

## 🧠 Titles: the curiosity formula

❌ `Rivals gameplay ep 4`
✅ `The Loadout Nobody Uses (It's Broken)`
✅ `I Played Rivals With Only the Worst Gun`
✅ `Rivals in 60 Seconds — Everything You Need`

**Formula:** *[specific thing] + [why I care]*. Numbers, stakes, or a twist. Under
60 characters so it doesn't get cut off.

---

## 🧠 Thumbnails: 3 rules

1. **Readable at phone size** — most viewers are on phones
2. **One focal point** — a face, a gun, a result. Not a busy screenshot.
3. **≤3 words** of text, huge

Make the thumbnail **before** you edit the video. If you can't make a compelling
thumbnail, you don't have a compelling video.

---

## 🧠 Description & tags (the 2-minute version)

- **First line = repeat the promise** (it shows in search)
- Add: what's in the video, 3–5 relevant search terms naturally
- **Tags matter little now** — title and thumbnail matter a lot

---

## 🧠 Shorts vs long videos

| | Shorts 📱 | Long videos ▶️ |
|---|---|---|
| Best for | Fast growth, discovery | Building a real audience |
| Your move | Clip your best 30 sec | Main channel content |

**Smart play:** make the long video, cut the best moment into a Short that points
to it. One effort, two products.

---

## 🧠 Schedule: pick it and keep it

- **One day, one time, every week.** e.g. Saturday 10am HK time (after school
  hours in Asia *and* US evening the night before 🌏)
- Consistency trains both the algorithm *and* your viewers
