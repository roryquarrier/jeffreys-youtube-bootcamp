# 🎮 Worked Example: "JF Rivals Lab"

Here's the whole course applied to a **Rivals channel**, start to finish — steal this structure.

---

## 📋 The channel plan

| Item | Decision |
|---|---|
| **Niche** | Rivals guides + tier lists (Roblox gaming) |
| **One viewer** | "The Rivals player stuck in Bronze who wants better aim" |
| **Name** | Something clean, ownable, spellable — e.g. *JF Rivals Lab* |
| **Format** | 4–8 min videos, voice + gameplay, no face |
| **Schedule** | Every Saturday, 10am HK time |

---

## 🎬 Video #1, fully scripted

**Title:** `Rivals in 5 Minutes — The Complete Beginner Guide`
**Thumbnail:** shocked face + "IN 5 MIN" (3 words)
**Hook (0:00–0:12):**

> *"If you're losing your first fight every match, this is the video for you.
> Five minutes, everything that matters: movement, your first weapon, and the one
> setting almost nobody changes."*

**Body (bullets on your screen while playing):**

1. **Movement first** — strafe while shooting; standing still = dying
2. **The starter weapon that wins fights** — show 3 clips of it working
3. **The setting** — sensitivity/crosshair change, show before/after
4. **Recap** — the 3 things, one sentence each

**Payoff (last 15 sec):**

> *"Movement, weapon, settings — that's the whole beginner game. Next Saturday:
> I'm ranking every weapon from trash to broken. Sub so you don't miss it."*

---

## 📅 The first month, realistically

| Week | Video | Why this one |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Beginner guide | Evergreen; new players search it forever |
| 2 | Weapon tier list | Clickable, arguable, comment fuel |
| 3 | "The loadout nobody uses" | Curiosity title, shows your knowledge |
| 4 | Update breakdown | Timely; rides the update search wave |

**Analytics ritual after each upload (5 min):** CTR low → new thumbnail. Big drop
at 0:30 → hook too slow. Nobody's fault. Just data.

---

## 🎤 Where the K-pop fits

Once the channel moves, sprinkle what makes it *yours*:

- Background music vibes (royalty-free only — see Grown-Ups note!)
- "Rivals but every kill plays a K-pop chorus" type formats
- A second channel later, or community posts

**Your taste is your differentiation.** Ten thousand people can make a Rivals
guide. Only you make *yours*.
