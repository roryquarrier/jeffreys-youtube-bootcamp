# 🧑‍🏫 For Grown-Ups

Jeffrey's course is finished — this page is for you. Five minutes, honest, no hype.

---

## 📜 The account rules (the important bit)

- **YouTube requires users to be 13+ to have their own account.** At 12, the
  correct setup is a **parent-managed account** (Google Family Link), with you
  holding the settings.
- **COPPA:** every upload must declare whether content is "made for kids."
  Mislabelling has real penalties — set it honestly per video. "Made for kids"
  disables comments and notifications; gaming content for teens is normally **not**
  marked as made-for-kids.
- **Monetisation** needs 1,000 subs + 4,000 watch hours (or 10M Shorts views/90
  days), **13+**, and an adult-managed AdSense account. Realistically 6–18 months
  away — treat any money talk as motivation, not a plan.

---

## 🛡️ Safety settings worth agreeing on together

- [ ] Parents are **administrators** on the channel account
- [ ] Comments set to **hold potentially inappropriate for review**
- [ ] No real name, school, address, or routine in videos or descriptions
- [ ] No in-person meetups with viewers, ever, without a parent
- [ ] DMs from strangers stay unanswered; anything odd → tell a parent, screenshot, block
- [ ] A weekly time budget for making videos that **school wins**

---

## ⚖️ Copyright, in one paragraph

Game footage is generally fine under publisher-friendly policies (Roblox explicitly
allows monetised videos). **Music is the trap:** K-pop tracks will get videos
claimed, muted or blocked. The habit to build now: **royalty-free music only**
(CapCut's library, YouTube Audio Library). Reaction videos should use short clips
with real commentary, not full songs. This one habit prevents most channel strikes.

---

## 🎯 What success looks like at 12

The realistic best case isn't money — it's a **portfolio**: a kid who can script,
edit, present, analyse data, and handle criticism. Those are career skills with or
without YouTube. The realistic worst case is burnout or an unmoderated comment
section — both prevented by the checkboxes above.

**The single most protective rule:** he tells you about the weird comment before he
replies to it. Keep that channel open and everything else is manageable.
