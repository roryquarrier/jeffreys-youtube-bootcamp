# 🎬 Step 3: Make Your First Video
**⏱️ 6:00 → 12:00 (6 minutes)**

## 🎯 Goal

Learn the **script formula** that keeps people watching — and the free tools that
make it look good.

---

## 🧠 The 3-part script formula

Every good video, from 60 seconds to 20 minutes, is the same shape:

### 1. HOOK (0–15 sec) — earn the click
Tell them exactly what they get:

> *"This is the loadout that got me from Bronze to Diamond — and the third weapon
> almost nobody uses."*

❌ Never start with "Hey guys, welcome back to my channel, today I'm going to…"
Viewers decide in **8 seconds.** The intro *is* the video starting.

### 2. BODY — deliver the promise, in order
- One point at a time
- Show, don't just tell (gameplay on screen while you explain)
- Cut every silence. If a second adds nothing, it goes.

### 3. PAYOFF + next step (last 15 sec)
Land the promise, then point forward:

> *"That's the loadout — drop yours in the comments. Next video: I'm testing the
> gun everyone says is trash."*

---

## 🧠 The retention rule 📉

**Retention** = the % of your video people watch. It's the biggest ranking signal.

| Enemy | Fix |
|---|---|
| Slow intro | Hook first, branding later |
| Rambling | Script bullet points, not paragraphs |
| Dead air | Cut. Cut. Cut. |
| No structure | "3 tips" beats "some tips" |

Watch your own video back. **Where did you get bored?** That's where viewers leave.

---

## 🧠 Voice or no voice?

- **Voice, no face:** perfect for gaming at 12. Cheap mic is fine; quiet room matters more.
- **Face cam:** builds connection — maybe later.
- **No voice at all:** possible (text + music) but much harder to grow.

**You don't need a fancy camera. You need clear audio and tight editing.**

---

## 🧠 The free toolkit (it's all you need)

| Job | Free tool |
|---|---|
| Record gameplay | **OBS Studio** (free, standard) |
| Edit | **CapCut** (easy) or **DaVinci Resolve** (pro) |
| Thumbnails | **Canva** (use their YouTube templates) |
| Audio cleanup | CapCut's built-in "enhance voice" |

💡 **Thumbnail rule:** big face or big object, 3 words max, readable at phone size.
Look at the Rivals thumbnails that got YOU to click. Copy that energy, not that
content.
